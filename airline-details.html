Airline Flight Performance Dashboard

Created an interactive dashboard from a 1,000-record airline flight dataset to analyze flight operations, passenger volume, airline performance, routes, flight status and aircraft usage.

The dataset contains 162,024 passengers across 90 routes, 7 airlines and 6 aircraft types. Flight status analysis shows 617 on-time flights, 193 delayed flights and 190 cancellations.

The dashboard highlights airline and route comparisons, passenger trends, flight status distribution, aircraft usage and operational patterns through KPI summaries, pivot analysis and visualizations.

AI tools used: JuliusAI, Quadratic AI, Bricks AI and Zebra AI for data quality review, analysis, visualizations and insight generation.
